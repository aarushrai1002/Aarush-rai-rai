    How it works

    From “I’m bored” to on the ground in minutes.

    1. Post or browse

      Start an activity with a place, time, and number of players, or browse what’s already happening nearby.

    2. Tap to join

      The spot counter updates instantly, so everyone knows how many more are needed.

    3. Show up and play

      Meet in person, play, and build a local community offline.

    Safety first. Events happen in public places only. Users sign up with a college email or phone number, and every event can be reported.
